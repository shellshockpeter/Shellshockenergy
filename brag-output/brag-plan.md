# Shell Shock Trainer — launch video plan

**What it is:** a competitive strength-ranking app (iOS, free). Log lifts, the Epley formula turns them into an estimated 1RM, and you climb 8 tiers x 3 divisions from Rust Mortal to Legendary Pantheon, in public, with a community around it.
**Angle:** "This isn't a fitness tracker." Rank is the product, so the real rank badges are the hero.
**Hook (first 2s):** the Rust Mortal badge slams in under "EARN YOUR RANK."
**Tone:** app-store clarity with a cinematic score. 9:16, 1080x1920, 30fps, 21.5s.
**Identity (from the live app):** black, #FACC15 yellow, #00CFFF cyan, Bebas Neue / DM Sans, Robot Reavers italic wordmark, the 8 real tier badges.
